About HYPERDASH

HYPERDASH is a pixel-art microgame game by Loosy Tale Studio, made in Haxe and OpenFL for SlapJam #2, whose theme was SPEED. Ninety seconds of flying, forty thousand light-years, or it’s free.

You drive for HyperDash Express, the cheapest courier in the galaxy. Your ship breaks every few seconds, and each fault is a microgame you have moments to fix: dodge rocks, rewire the reactor, dock at full burn. Every fix makes you faster, and the music speeds up with you.

The delivery clock only runs while you’re fixing things. Fast clears earn tips; mistakes cost cargo and time. When your pilot goes down, the next one pulls the parcel from the wreck and carries on. Nova, Vex, Captain Rust, Kade and PIP each bring their own story, ship and perk.

It plays in the browser with mouse, touch or keyboard, and screen shake and flashes can be turned off. Character portraits by Batareya.

Features

  • 14 microgames across four sectors, plus a Hyperlane boss
  • Story, Endless and Training modes
  • Five pilots with their own story, ship and perk
  • A soundtrack that follows your speed
  • Mouse, touch or keyboard
