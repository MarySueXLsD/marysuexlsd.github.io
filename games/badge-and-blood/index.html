About Badge & Blood

Badge & Blood is a bodycam first-person shooter in development at Loosy Tale Studio, built in Unreal Engine 5. The camera sits on your chest: a 120° fisheye lens, the weapon on the centre line, a low golden-hour sun, and no reticle and no HUD. Every mouse move turns the picture in the same frame, while the gun swings ahead and the view settles onto it.

The world reacts the way it should. Glass shatters pane by pane and the bullet carries on through it. One shot bursts a cardboard box into about 140 scraps that flutter down and lie where they land. Bullet holes are moved onto the surface you can actually see, and puddles splash instead of leaving holes.

Movement is physical. Lean 17° round a corner, slide, roll out of a fall, and climb through any window a body really fits through. Cars can be driven, shot up through the windscreen and repaired part by part, and the garage lets you tune the engine, gearbox, paint, glass and armour.

For multiplayer, press F6 for a one-on-one duel over LAN, in two container yards built for it: three staggered lanes, stacks that do not stand squarely, and no sight line longer than about 17 metres.

Features

  • 120° fisheye chest camera, no reticle, no HUD
  • Lean, slide, safe roll and window climbing
  • Glass that shatters pane by pane, cardboard that bursts
  • Drivable cars with per-part damage and a tuning garage
  • A bag where every item has a rarity and a price, and a workshop bench for attachments and skins
  • One-on-one LAN duels in two purpose-built container yards
