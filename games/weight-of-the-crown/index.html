About The Weight of the Crown

The Weight of the Crown is a choice-driven kingdom game by Loosy Tale Studio, written in Daslang on the EdenSpark engine for EdenSpark Game Jam #2. King Edwin is dead. You killed him. You are Gregor, the usurper, and Loria is yours for as long as you can hold it.

The realm is still bleeding: plague in the streets, empty granaries, soldiers waiting on pay, and a church that hasn’t decided whether your crown is holy or cursed. Every morning brings another petitioner and another crisis.

Courtiers and commoners come before the throne, one a day, with problems only a monarch can answer. Every answer moves nine pillars of power: the people, the church, the army, the treasury, health, loyalty, the nobility, food and the succession. Survive 365 days of rule.

Features

  • One petitioner and one decision a day
  • Nine pillars of power that push on each other
  • 365 days of rule to survive
  • A cast of courtiers, clergy and commoners
  • Its own soundtrack and painted cut-scenes
