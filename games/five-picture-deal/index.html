About Five-Picture Deal

Five-Picture Deal is a first-person stunt shooter by Loosy Tale Studio, made in Unreal Engine 5.8 for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam. You signed a five-picture deal, the director wants every stunt for real, and there is no stunt double.

Wall-run, slide, flip and swing on the hook through three action movies: feudal Japan in The Last Scroll, the Wild West in Dry Gulch, and a frozen city in Whiteout. Every stunt is also your armour: mid-stunt, bullets can’t touch you. Die, and it’s CUT!: the tape rewinds and you go again.

Land the director’s three notes in one take for a DIRECTOR’S CUT!, win medals in side scenes, and take down each picture’s villain.

Made by the Loosy Tale Studio: MarySue, with co-author Koshhh. Built with Unreal Engine 5.8, Claude (Claude Code) and SDXL.

Features

  • Wall-runs, slides, flips and rope swings
  • Stunts double as armour
  • Three movies: feudal Japan, the Wild West, a frozen city
  • Director’s notes, side scenes and medals
  • Die and the tape rewinds
