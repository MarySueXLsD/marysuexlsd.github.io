About The Elder’s Saga

The Elder’s Saga is a hardcore turn-based isometric tactics game by Loosy Tale Studio, written in Rust from the Loosy Tale design documents. In ancient times, when humans first arrived in this world, the Elders already dwelled here.

Every order is yours. Move, attack and shoot barrels; use your squad’s skills; set overwatch; and spend action points on Steel Nerves to steady their Psycho Stability. Cover matters: hover a tile and a shield stands on each protected edge, tall for full cover and short for half.

The current build contains the Prologue and Act I, plus the Endless Stand mode, where you hold as long as you can against wave after wave.

Features

  • Hardcore turn-based isometric tactics
  • Full and half cover, overwatch and line of sight
  • Psycho Stability and Steel Nerves
  • Prologue, Act I and the Endless Stand mode
  • A single Windows executable, no installation
