About Rewind

Rewind is a retro horror game by Loosy Tale Studio, made in Unreal Engine 5.8 for Retro Horror Jam 2026. Junction 4 went silent, and you are the inspector sent down to find the crew.

Your chest bodycam never stops recording. Hold R to replay the last 10 seconds: the tape shows what your eyes missed. Rewind to see the Lineman. It only exists on tape, and every replay drains your battery.

Stay in the light. It moves only in the dark. Click your dying flashlight back to life, find the lift key and get back to the surface.

Controls: WASD to move, Shift to run, Ctrl to crouch, Q and E to lean, F to interact, left mouse for the flashlight, right mouse to focus, R to rewind, I for the inventory and M for the map. Gamepad supported. Built with the Retro Horror Engine template by RetroGig; environment Underground Tunnel by MacKenzie Shirk.

Features

  • Replay the last 10 seconds on your bodycam
  • An enemy that only exists on tape
  • A battery that every replay drains
  • A dying flashlight and a light that keeps you safe
  • 10 to 15 minutes long
