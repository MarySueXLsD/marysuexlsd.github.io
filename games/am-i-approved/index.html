About Am I Approved?

Am I Approved? is a bureaucratic desk sim by Loosy Tale Studio, set in the Republic of Loria, circa 1967. You were hired as employee #447-B to approve loans, reject loans, and look busy between loans. Management has full confidence in you.

Clients arrive at your desk with stories, passports and paperwork that doesn’t always add up. Your job is to listen, inspect, compare, and decide who gets the money.

Talk to clients with small talk, pointed questions and the occasional lie you pretend not to notice. Drag passports, ID cards and contracts onto your desk and cross-check every detail. Search the citizen database, edit records and file loan applications on a green-screen bank computer.

It is a document-heavy game inspired by bureaucratic sims like Papers, Please, with dry workplace humour baked into the employee handbook and the client encounters. Made with Haxe and OpenFL for The $20K Coolmath Game Jam 2026.

Features

  • Talk to clients and catch the lies
  • Cross-check passports, ID cards and contracts
  • A green-screen bank computer with a citizen database
  • Dry workplace humour
