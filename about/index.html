[ THE PERSON BEHIND THE CURTAIN ]

Hi, I’m MarySue.

Viktar Syanau: game designer and programmer, 11 years in, and the one-person heart of Loosy Tale Studio.

Say hiitch.io ↗GitHub ↗

I build games where systems and story collide: small worlds with sharp rules, strange people, and choices that keep looking back at you.

Loosy Tale Studio is where those games live. Most of them start as jam entries and keep growing after the jam ends.

Games in Unreal Engine 5, Haxe and OpenFL, Rust, Daslang and C#, from short jam games played in the browser to a bodycam shooter and a turn-based tactics game. Along the way I build my own tools: a game engine, mods, a VS Code extension, a Twitch bot and a local AI assistant.
