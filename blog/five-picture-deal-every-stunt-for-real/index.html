Five-Picture Deal: every stunt for real

A first-person stunt shooter made in Unreal Engine 5.8 for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam.

By MarySue · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 min read

● REC · FIVE-PICTURE DEAL25 NOV 20261 MIN READJAM · FIVE-PICTURE-DEAL · UNREAL

You signed a five-picture deal. The director wants every stunt for real, and there is no stunt double.

Five-Picture Deal is a first-person stunt shooter by Loosy Tale Studio, made in Unreal Engine 5.8 for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam by MarySue with co-author Koshhh.

Three movies

Wall-run, slide, flip and swing on the hook through three action movies: feudal Japan in The Last Scroll, the Wild West in Dry Gulch, and a frozen city in Whiteout.

Stunts are armour

Every stunt is also your armour: mid-stunt, bullets can’t touch you. Die, and it’s CUT!: the tape rewinds and you go again.

Land the director’s three notes in one take for a DIRECTOR’S CUT!

Meet the director

Win medals in side scenes, take down each picture’s villain, and keep the director happy.
