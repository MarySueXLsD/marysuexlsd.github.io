Now filming: Badge & Blood

A bodycam shooter in Unreal Engine 5: no reticle, no HUD, just the camera on your chest.

By MarySue · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 min read

● REC · BADGE & BLOOD25 NOV 20261 MIN READDEVLOG · BADGE-AND-BLOOD · UNREAL

A bodycam shooter in Unreal Engine 5: a chest-mounted fisheye camera, no reticle and no HUD, glass that breaks pane by pane, and one-on-one duels over LAN.

The camera sits on your chest: a 120° fisheye lens, the weapon on the centre line and a low golden-hour sun. Every mouse move turns the picture in the same frame, while the gun swings ahead and the view settles onto it.

A world that reacts

Glass shatters pane by pane and the bullet carries on through it. One shot bursts a cardboard box into about 140 scraps that flutter down and lie where they land. Bullet holes are moved onto the surface you can actually see, and puddles splash instead of leaving holes.

Cars you can wreck and fix

Cars can be driven, shot up through the windscreen and repaired part by part, and the garage lets you tune the engine, gearbox, paint, glass and armour.

Duels over LAN

Press F6

For multiplayer, press F6 for a one-on-one duel over LAN, in two container yards built for it: three staggered lanes, stacks that do not stand squarely, and no sight line longer than about 17 metres.
