HYPERDASH: a delivery made of microgames

Five couriers, one parcel, forty thousand light-years. Made in Haxe and OpenFL for SlapJam #2.

By MarySue · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 min read

● REC · HYPERDASH25 NOV 20261 MIN READJAM · HYPERDASH · HAXE

Ninety seconds of flying, forty thousand light-years, or it’s free.

HYPERDASH is a pixel-art microgame game by Loosy Tale Studio, made in Haxe and OpenFL for SlapJam #2, whose theme was SPEED. It plays in the browser with mouse, touch or keyboard.

Fix it, faster

You drive for HyperDash Express, the cheapest courier in the galaxy. Your ship breaks every few seconds, and each fault is a microgame you have moments to fix: dodge rocks, rewire the reactor, dock at full burn. Every fix makes you faster, and the music speeds up with you.

The parcel goes on

Five pilots

When your pilot goes down, the next one pulls the parcel from the wreck and carries on. Nova, Vex, Captain Rust, Kade and PIP each bring their own story, ship and perk.

The delivery clock only runs while you’re fixing things. Fast clears earn tips; mistakes cost cargo and time. Character portraits by Batareya.
