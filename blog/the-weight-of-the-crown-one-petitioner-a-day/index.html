The Weight of the Crown: one petitioner a day

A kingdom of impossible choices, written in Daslang on the EdenSpark engine.

By MarySue · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 min read

♛
● REC · THE WEIGHT OF THE CROWN25 NOV 20261 MIN READDEVLOG · WEIGHT-OF-THE-CROWN · DASLANG

King Edwin is dead. You killed him. Now keep the throne, one impossible choice at a time.

The Weight of the Crown is a choice-driven kingdom game by Loosy Tale Studio, written in Daslang on the EdenSpark engine for EdenSpark Game Jam #2. You are Gregor, the usurper, and Loria is yours for as long as you can hold it.

A realm still bleeding

Plague in the streets, empty granaries, soldiers waiting on pay, and a church that hasn’t decided whether your crown is holy or cursed. Every morning brings another petitioner and another crisis.

One a day

Courtiers and commoners come before the throne, one a day, with problems only a monarch can answer.

Nine pillars

  • The people
  • The church
  • The army
  • The treasury
  • Health
  • Loyalty
  • The nobility
  • Food
  • The succession

Every answer moves all nine. Survive 365 days of rule.
