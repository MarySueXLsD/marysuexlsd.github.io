Rewind: the tape sees what you missed

A short retro horror game made in Unreal Engine 5.8 for Retro Horror Jam 2026.

By MarySue · 25 Nov 2026 · 1 min read

● REC · REWIND25 NOV 20261 MIN READJAM · REWIND · HORROR

Junction 4 went silent, and you are the inspector sent down to find the crew.

Rewind is a retro horror game by Loosy Tale Studio, made in Unreal Engine 5.8 for Retro Horror Jam 2026. It takes 10 to 15 minutes to play.

Hold R

Your chest bodycam never stops recording. Hold R to replay the last 10 seconds: the tape shows what your eyes missed. Rewind to see the Lineman. It only exists on tape, and every replay drains your battery.

[ THE RULE ]

Stay in the light. It moves only in the dark.

Click your dying flashlight back to life, find the lift key and get back to the surface.

Built with the Retro Horror Engine template by RetroGig; environment Underground Tunnel by MacKenzie Shirk. No generative AI was used.
