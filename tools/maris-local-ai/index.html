About Maris Local AI

Maris Local AI is a private AI assistant that runs entirely on your PC. It brings together chat, a coding agent, PC help that asks before it changes anything, memory that learns, web browsing, and local image and video generation.

Because it runs locally, your files and conversations stay on your own machine.
